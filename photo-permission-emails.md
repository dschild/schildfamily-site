# Photo permission request emails

Three short drafts — one per source — for asking permission to use a photo on
schildfamily.com's "Notable Schilds" section. Each explains the nonprofit,
non-commercial, credited use and asks for a simple yes/no. Fill in the
bracketed placeholders before sending.

---

## 1. Alfred Schild — UT Austin Physics History site

**To:** the site maintainer/contact listed on utphysicshistory.net (check the
site's About/Contact page for a name and address)
**Subject:** Permission to use an Alfred Schild photo on a small family history site

Hello,

I run a small, noncommercial family history website (schildfamily.com) that
includes a short page about people who happen to share the Schild surname,
including physicist Alfred Schild. I came across your page on Alfred Ernst
Schild and would like to ask permission to use one photo from it — [describe
which one, e.g., "the formal portrait at the top of the page" or "the sepia
portrait captioned 'Alfred Schild, (1921–1977)'"].

A few details that might help:
- The site is a free, ongoing family archive project, not a commercial
  venture.
- The photo would appear as a small thumbnail with a caption crediting the
  source and photographer (if known).
- I'm happy to link back to your page, or to remove the photo at any time if
  you'd prefer.

Would you be willing to grant permission for this use? If the photo actually
originated elsewhere (for example, the *Austin American-Statesman*), I'd
appreciate knowing that too, so I can ask the right party.

Thank you for maintaining such a thorough record of Professor Schild's life
and work.

Best,
David Schild
schildfamily.com

## 2. Erwin Schild — The Globe and Mail

like to include this photo as a small credited thumbnail, with full
photographer/publication credit and a link back to your article.

Could you let me know whether this use would be permitted, and if there's a
fee or a formal licensing process I should go through? I'm glad to follow
whatever process you require.

Thank you,
David Schild
schildfamily.com

schildfamily.com
