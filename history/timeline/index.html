History/Timeline

Schild Family History

Timeline

Dated events from the documented record, earliest to latest. Each links to the story, people, and record behind it.

All timeline events

  1. 1839

    Ann Jacobs Arrives in South Australia

    Ann Jacobs, later Ann Horrocks, arrives in South Australia at about twenty one years old. An original land grant records her purchase of the property that would become Moorooroo.

    1839 Moorooroo

    Explore Story →
  2. 1847

    The Moorooroo Shiraz Vines Are Planted

    Ann Jacobs plants Shiraz vines at Moorooroo, eleven years after the founding of South Australia. Four rows of that planting still survive today.

    1847 Moorooroo

    Explore Story →
  3. 1854

    Theodor Schild Receives Land Near Grapetown

    Theodor Schild was among the early settlers around Grapetown, Texas, and received a land deed on February 13, 1854.

    February 13, 1854 Theodor Schild Grapetown, Texas

    Explore Story →
  4. 1856

    Urs Schild and Dr Joseph Girard Found a Watch Movement Business in Grenchen

    Urs Schild and Dr Joseph Girard found a small watch movement business in Grenchen, Switzerland. It grew over the following decades into what became known as Eterna.

    November 7, 1856 Urs Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  5. 1860

    August and Theodor Schild Appear in the Census

    The 1860 U.S. Census for Fredericksburg, Gillespie County records Theodor Schild, about 42, a Saxony-born farmer — and immediately adjacent, August Schild, about 39, a Saxony-born merchant with a household of his own.

    1860 August Schild & Theodor Schild Fredericksburg, Texas

    Explore Story →
  6. 1868

    August Schild Named Postmaster of Fredericksburg

    August Schild was recorded as postmaster of Fredericksburg in 1868, a role tied to his standing as a local merchant.

    1868 August Schild Fredericksburg, Texas

    Explore Story →
  7. 1869

    August Schild Continues as Postmaster

    August Schild again appears as Fredericksburg’s postmaster in 1869. A later biographical source identifies Lena Schild as his daughter.

    1869 August Schild Fredericksburg, Texas

    Explore Story →
  8. 1878

    The Business Begins Producing Complete Watches

    By the late 1870s the Grenchen workshop is producing complete watches, not only the movements inside them. The exact year is approximate.

    By the late 1870s Urs Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  9. 1888

    Urs Schild Dies; the Next Generation Takes Over

    Leadership of the Grenchen business passes to Urs Schild’s brother Adolf, his widow, and his sons Max and Theodor Schild.

    1888 Urs Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  10. 1896

    Adolf Schild Founds A. Schild SA

    Adolf Schild leaves the family firm to found his own movement manufacturer, A. Schild SA, later part of the lineage that led to ETA.

    1896 Adolf Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  11. 1899

    Theodor Schild Leads the Company Into the Wristwatch Era

    Theodor Schild takes charge of the family business and leads it for more than three decades, guiding it into wristwatches.

    1899 Theodor Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  12. 1905

    The Eterna Trademark Is Registered

    The name Eterna is registered as a trademark in Switzerland on November 11, 1905, and begins appearing publicly soon after.

    November 11, 1905 Theodor Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  13. 1916

    The Kleemann Vineyard Is Planted

    The Kleemann vineyard, high on the eastern hills above Rowland Flat, is planted. Its bush vines are still hand picked today.

    1916 Kleemann

    Explore Story →
  14. 1920

    Erwin Schild Born in Cologne Mülheim

    Erwin Schild is born on March 9, 1920, in Cologne Mülheim, Germany, to businessman Hermann Schild and his wife Hetti.

    March 9, 1920 Erwin Schild Cologne Mülheim, Germany

    Explore Story →
  15. 1925

    August W. Schild Jr. Born in Waco, Texas

    August W. Schild Jr. was born in Waco, Texas, on March 1, 1925.

    March 1, 1925 August W. Schild Jr. Waco, Texas

    Explore Story →
  16. 1932

    Eterna and ETA Become Separate Companies

    During a difficult period for Swiss watch manufacturers, the watch business, Eterna, and the movement business, ETA, become separate companies, both still rooted in Grenchen.

    1932 Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  17. 1938

    The November Pogrom Reaches Würzburg

    A mob attacks Erwin Schild’s seminary dormitory in Würzburg, and he witnesses the seminary building and its library burning the following day.

    November 9–10, 1938 Erwin Schild Würzburg, Germany

    Explore Story →
  18. 1938

    Erwin Schild Imprisoned at Dachau

    At eighteen years old, Erwin Schild is imprisoned at Dachau concentration camp in the roundup that follows the November pogrom.

    November 12, 1938 Erwin Schild Dachau, Germany

    Explore Story →
  19. 1938

    Released From Dachau

    Erwin Schild is released from Dachau after a little over four weeks, on the condition that he emigrate from Germany.

    December 13, 1938 Erwin Schild Dachau, Germany

    Explore Story →
  20. 1939

    Escape to the Netherlands, Then Britain

    Erwin Schild leaves Nazi Germany, traveling first to the Netherlands and then to Britain, where he resumes his religious studies in London.

    1939 Erwin Schild London, England

    Explore Story →
  21. 1940

    Kurt Schild Emigrates to the United States

    Erwin’s older brother Kurt Schild emigrates to the United States, described in one account as among the last Jews able to leave Germany.

    1940 Kurt Schild Cologne Mülheim, Germany

    Explore Story →
  22. 1940

    Classified an Enemy Alien and Sent to Canada

    British authorities classify Erwin Schild, a refugee from Nazi persecution, as an enemy alien and deport him to Canada, where he is interned.

    1940 Erwin Schild Wartime Internment in Canada

    Explore Story →
  23. 1941

    Hermann, Hetti, and Margot Schild Deported to Riga

    Erwin’s parents Hermann and Hetti Schild, along with his sister Margot, are deported to the Riga ghetto. Hermann and Hetti do not survive; Margot survives further camps and a death march.

    1941 Hermann Schild, Hetti Schild & Margot Schild Cologne Mülheim, Germany

    Explore Story →
  24. 1942

    Released From Internment

    Erwin Schild is released from internment in Canada, with assistance from the Canadian Jewish Congress, and goes on to study in Toronto.

    February 1942 Erwin Schild Toronto, Ontario

    Explore Story →
  25. WWII

    Serves with the 513th Bombardment Squadron, 376th Heavy Bombardment Group

    August W. Schild Jr. served as a Technical Sergeant and radio operator/gunner with the 513th Bombardment Squadron of the 376th Heavy Bombardment Group (“The Liberandos”), which flew B-24 Liberators from the Mediterranean theater into Europe. Exact service dates not yet verified.

    World War II August W. Schild Jr. Mediterranean Theater

    Explore Story →
  26. 1947

    Ordained as a Rabbi

    Erwin Schild completes his rabbinical studies in Toronto and is ordained. Sources mostly agree on 1947, though one German source instead gives December 1944.

    1947 Erwin Schild Toronto, Ontario

    Explore Story →
  27. 1947

    Becomes Rabbi of Adath Israel Congregation

    Erwin Schild becomes rabbi of Adath Israel Congregation in Toronto, then numbering roughly one hundred fifty families. He remains its rabbi for more than forty years.

    September 1947 Erwin Schild Adath Israel Congregation

    Explore Story →
  28. 1950s

    Charles Ray Schild Races at Playland Park

    The earliest documented racing in this Houston Schild family: Charles Ray Schild competes at the stock car track inside Playland Park. No exact date has been confirmed, so an approximate decade is used here.

    Approximate, 1950s Charles Ray Schild Playland Park, Houston

    Explore Story →
  29. 1952

    Ben and Alma Schild Settle Narrow Road

    Ben and Alma Schild purchase and settle a property along historic Narrow Road near Rowland Flat, beginning the family’s documented Barossa vineyard story.

    1952 Ben Schild & Alma Schild Narrow Road

    Explore Story →
  30. 1956

    Ben Schild Dies; Ed Schild Takes Over at Sixteen

    Ben Schild dies unexpectedly. His son Ed takes over the family property at sixteen years old and leads its expansion over the following decades.

    1956 Ben Schild & Ed Schild Rowland Flat, South Australia

    Explore Story →
  31. 1972

    Jerry Schild Begins Racing at Meyer Speedway

    Jerry Schild starts racing at Meyer Speedway in Houston, the beginning of a career that would reach NASCAR's top series two years later.

    1972 Jerry Schild Meyer Speedway, Houston

    Explore Story →
  32. 1974

    Jerry Schild Races in NASCAR's Top Series

    Jerry Schild makes five starts in NASCAR's top series, recording an eighth place finish at Darlington Raceway, the high point of his brief top level career.

    1974 Jerry Schild Darlington Raceway

    Explore Story →
  33. 1989

    Retires as Congregational Rabbi

    After more than forty years as its rabbi, Erwin Schild retires from the pulpit at Adath Israel Congregation and is named Rabbi Emeritus.

    1989 Erwin Schild Adath Israel Congregation

    Explore Story →
  34. 1993

    The Family Purchases the Chenin Blanc Vineyard

    The family purchases a vineyard later known as Workshop, at the time called the Chenin Blanc vineyard.

    1993 Workshop

    Explore Story →
  35. 1999

    Michael Schild Builds a Workshop Nearby

    Michael Schild builds a new workshop just down the road from the Chenin Blanc vineyard, which becomes known afterward simply as Workshop.

    1999 Michael Schild Workshop

    Explore Story →
  36. 2001

    Angus Brae Is Purchased

    The family purchases a vineyard north of Lyndoch, naming it Angus Brae for Ed Schild’s grandson Angus and the Scottish word for a steep hillside.

    December 2001 Ed Schild & Angus Schild Angus Brae

    Explore Story →
  37. 2001

    Member of the Order of Canada

    Erwin Schild is made a Member of the Order of Canada for his decades of work improving Christian and Jewish dialogue and reconciliation.

    Awarded May 30, 2001 Erwin Schild Toronto, Ontario

    Explore Story →
  38. 2003

    Jerry Schild Wins a Texas State Championship

    Decades after his NASCAR season, Jerry Schild wins a state championship racing at Houston area short tracks.

    2003 Jerry Schild

    Explore Story →
  39. 2006

    Jerry Schild Wins a Houston Motorsports Park Track Championship

    Jerry Schild wins a track championship at Houston Motorsports Park, the track his niece Gina would later take over running.

    2006 Jerry Schild Houston Motorsports Park

    Explore Story →
  40. 2009

    Six Weeks After Surgery

    Jerry Schild donates a kidney to his sister Linda, then returns to racing only weeks after the surgery.

    2009 Jerry Schild & Linda Schild High

    Explore Story →
  41. 2012

    Jerry Schild Dies

    Jerry Schild dies, closing out a racing career that spanned four decades, from a Houston short track debut in 1972 to a NASCAR Cup season in 1974.

    2012 Jerry Schild

    Explore Story →
  42. 2019

    Gina Schild Knowles Takes Over Houston Motorsports Park

    Gina Schild Knowles takes over the lease of Houston Motorsports Park, moving her branch of the family from driving into running the track itself.

    2019 Gina Schild Knowles Houston Motorsports Park

    Explore Story →
  43. 2020

    Houston Motorsports Park Regains NASCAR Sanctioning

    Under Gina Schild Knowles, Houston Motorsports Park regains NASCAR sanctioning, restoring the track's standing within the sport her family had raced in for generations.

    Approximate, around 2020 Gina Schild Knowles Houston Motorsports Park

    Explore Story →
  44. 2022

    The Family Returns Its Focus to the Vineyards

    The family announces plans to sell the Schild Estate winery. The winery moves to Atlas Wines and the wine brand to CW Wines, while the family keeps its vineyards. Ed Schild steps back from leading the business and his son Michael takes over.

    Announced 2022 Ed Schild & Michael Schild Rowland Flat, South Australia

    Explore Story →
  45. 2024

    Erwin Schild Dies in Toronto

    Erwin Schild dies in Toronto on January 6, 2024, at the age of 103.

    January 6, 2024 Erwin Schild Toronto, Ontario

    Explore Story →
