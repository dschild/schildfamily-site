History/Timeline

Schild Family History

Timeline

Dated events from the documented record, earliest to latest. Each links to the story, people, and record behind it.

All timeline events

  1. 1854

    Theodor Schild Receives Land Near Grapetown

    Theodor Schild was among the early settlers around Grapetown, Texas, and received a land deed on February 13, 1854.

    February 13, 1854 Theodor Schild Grapetown, Texas

    Explore Story →
  2. 1860

    August and Theodor Schild Appear in the Census

    The 1860 U.S. Census for Fredericksburg, Gillespie County records Theodor Schild, about 42, a Saxony-born farmer — and immediately adjacent, August Schild, about 39, a Saxony-born merchant with a household of his own.

    1860 August Schild & Theodor Schild Fredericksburg, Texas

    Explore Story →
  3. 1868

    August Schild Named Postmaster of Fredericksburg

    August Schild was recorded as postmaster of Fredericksburg in 1868, a role tied to his standing as a local merchant.

    1868 August Schild Fredericksburg, Texas

    Explore Story →
  4. 1869

    August Schild Continues as Postmaster

    August Schild again appears as Fredericksburg’s postmaster in 1869. A later biographical source identifies Lena Schild as his daughter.

    1869 August Schild Fredericksburg, Texas

    Explore Story →
  5. 1925

    August W. Schild Jr. Born in Waco, Texas

    August W. Schild Jr. was born in Waco, Texas, on March 1, 1925.

    March 1, 1925 August W. Schild Jr. Waco, Texas

    Explore Story →
  6. WWII

    Serves with the 513th Bombardment Squadron, 376th Heavy Bombardment Group

    August W. Schild Jr. served as a Technical Sergeant and radio operator/gunner with the 513th Bombardment Squadron of the 376th Heavy Bombardment Group (“The Liberandos”), which flew B-24 Liberators from the Mediterranean theater into Europe. Exact service dates not yet verified.

    World War II August W. Schild Jr. Mediterranean Theater

    Explore Story →
  7. 1856

    Urs Schild and Dr Joseph Girard Found a Watch Movement Business in Grenchen

    Urs Schild and Dr Joseph Girard found a small watch movement business in Grenchen, Switzerland. It grew over the following decades into what became known as Eterna.

    November 7, 1856 Urs Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  8. 1878

    The Business Begins Producing Complete Watches

    By the late 1870s the Grenchen workshop is producing complete watches, not only the movements inside them. The exact year is approximate.

    By the late 1870s Urs Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  9. 1888

    Urs Schild Dies; the Next Generation Takes Over

    Leadership of the Grenchen business passes to Urs Schild’s brother Adolf, his widow, and his sons Max and Theodor Schild.

    1888 Urs Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  10. 1896

    Adolf Schild Founds A. Schild SA

    Adolf Schild leaves the family firm to found his own movement manufacturer, A. Schild SA, later part of the lineage that led to ETA.

    1896 Adolf Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  11. 1899

    Theodor Schild Leads the Company Into the Wristwatch Era

    Theodor Schild takes charge of the family business and leads it for more than three decades, guiding it into wristwatches.

    1899 Theodor Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  12. 1905

    The Eterna Trademark Is Registered

    The name Eterna is registered as a trademark in Switzerland on November 11, 1905, and begins appearing publicly soon after.

    November 11, 1905 Theodor Schild Grenchen, Switzerland

    Explore Story →
  13. 1932

    Eterna and ETA Become Separate Companies

    During a difficult period for Swiss watch manufacturers, the watch business, Eterna, and the movement business, ETA, become separate companies, both still rooted in Grenchen.

    1932 Grenchen, Switzerland
