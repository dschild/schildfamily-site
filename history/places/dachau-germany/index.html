History/Places/Dachau

Bavaria, Germany

Dachau

Site of the Dachau concentration camp, where Erwin Schild was imprisoned for a little over four weeks in 1938.

Wider Schild Story

This place is connected to a wider Schild surname story, not a confirmed part of our direct family line.

The former roll call yard at Dachau, photographed as it appears today, preserved as a memorial and historical site.

Photograph by Netha Hussain, 2021, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.

Dachau, near Munich, was the site of the first Nazi concentration camp, opened in 1933. Erwin Schild was imprisoned there from November 12 to December 13, 1938, at the age of eighteen, following the November pogrom and his arrest in Würzburg.

This page describes the place as it connects to Erwin’s own documented month there, drawn from the camp memorial’s own Remembrance Book, rather than attempting a general history of the camp as a whole.
