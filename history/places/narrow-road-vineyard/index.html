History/Places/Narrow Road

Rowland Flat, Barossa Valley

Narrow Road

The vineyard site adjacent to the original Schild property, settled in 1952.

Wider Schild Story

This place is connected to a wider Schild surname story in Barossa Valley wine history, not a confirmed part of our direct family line.

Narrow Road sits high on the hills behind the township of Rowland Flat, adjacent to the original Schild property purchased and settled by Ben and Alma Schild in 1952. Of the family’s eleven vineyard sites, this is the one most directly tied to the beginning of the family’s Barossa story.
