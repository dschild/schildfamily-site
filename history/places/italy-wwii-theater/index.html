Italy (WWII Operations)

Following the North African campaign, the 376th Heavy Bombardment Group relocated to airfields in Southern Italy, from which it continued long-range missions into occupied Europe.

This entry marks Southern Italy as part of the 376th Heavy Bombardment Group’s wartime area of operations — the unit August W. Schild Jr. served in — not a personal residence. It reflects unit history rather than a documented account of August’s individual movements.
