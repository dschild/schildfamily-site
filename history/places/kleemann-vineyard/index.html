History/Places/Kleemann

Eastern hills above Rowland Flat, Barossa Valley

Kleemann

Planted in 1916, its bush vines still hand picked today.

Wider Schild Story

This place is connected to a wider Schild surname story in Barossa Valley wine history, not a confirmed part of our direct family line.

Kleemann sits high on the eastern hills overlooking Rowland Flat, planted in 1916. Its bush vines grow untrellised, exposed to the elements, and are still hand picked, much as they have been for more than a century.
