McLennan County, Texas

Waco, Texas

The city on the Brazos River in Central Texas where August W. Schild Jr. was born on March 1, 1925.

Waco, the seat of McLennan County in Central Texas, is the birthplace of August W. Schild Jr., born there on March 1, 1925 — two decades before his World War II service with the 376th Heavy Bombardment Group.
