Houston Motorsports Park is a three eighths mile, high banked asphalt oval in Houston. Jerry Schild won a track championship here in 2006.

In 2019, his niece Gina Schild Knowles took over the track's lease, and it worked its way back to NASCAR sanctioning around 2020 under her leadership, alongside race director Mason Mitchell.
