Gillespie County, Texas

Grapetown, Texas

A small German settlement a few miles from Fredericksburg, where Theodor Schild received a land deed in 1854.

Grapetown is a small community in Gillespie County, a few miles south of Fredericksburg. Theodor Schild was among its early settlers, receiving a land deed there on February 13, 1854.
