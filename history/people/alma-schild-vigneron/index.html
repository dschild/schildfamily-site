Alma Schild, with her husband Ben, purchased and settled a property along historic Narrow Road near Rowland Flat in the Barossa Valley in 1952, beginning the family’s documented vineyard story there.

No confirmed photograph of Alma Schild has been located for this archive, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
