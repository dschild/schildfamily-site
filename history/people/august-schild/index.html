August Schild

b. c. 1821, Saxony, Germany · Merchant & U.S. Postmaster of Fredericksburg

Born
c. 1821, Saxony, Germany
Occupation
Merchant; U.S. Postmaster (1868–1869)
Places
Fredericksburg, Texas
Children
Lena Schild

August Schild appears in the 1860 U.S. Census for Fredericksburg, Gillespie County, Texas, about age 39, born in Saxony, Germany, and working as a merchant with a household of his own. Immediately adjacent in the same census is Theodor Schild — also Saxony-born and close in age — though whether the two men were related has not been established (see below).

By 1868, August Schild was recorded as postmaster of Fredericksburg, a position he continued to hold in 1869. A later biographical source identifies Lena Schild as his daughter.

A Family Mystery

Were August and Theodor Schild brothers?

They appear beside one another in the 1860 census, were close in age, and were both born in Saxony, but their exact relationship has not yet been proven.
