Michael Schild, son of Ed Schild, built a new workshop near the family’s Chenin Blanc vineyard in 1999. Afterward, the vineyard became known simply as Workshop, a name that has stuck ever since.

Around 2022, as the family announced plans to sell the Schild Estate winery and return its primary focus to grapegrowing, Michael took over leading the business from his father Ed. No confirmed photograph of Michael Schild has been located for this archive, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
