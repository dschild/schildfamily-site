August W. Schild Jr. was born in Waco, Texas, on March 1, 1925. During World War II he served as a radio operator and gunner, reaching the rank of Technical Sergeant, with the 513th Bombardment Squadron of the 376th Heavy Bombardment Group — nicknamed “The Liberandos” — which flew B-24 Liberator bombers from the Mediterranean theater into occupied Europe.

His individual crew assignment, aircraft, and personal mission history have not yet been identified (see below). What is documented comes from the group’s enlisted personnel roster and his obituary.
