Father of Jerry and Larry Schild

Charles Ray Schild is the earliest racer documented in this Houston Schild family, competing at the stock car track inside Playland Park, a Houston amusement park open from 1940 to 1966.

No detailed record of his individual results has surfaced. This page stays close to what is confirmed: that racing in this family began with him, and that his sons Larry and Jerry both carried it forward.
