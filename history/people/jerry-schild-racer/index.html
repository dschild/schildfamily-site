Jerry Schild

1954–2012 · NASCAR Cup Series Driver · Meyer Speedway · Houston Motorsports Park

Family
Son of Charles Ray Schild; Larry Schild's brother; Linda Schild High's brother; Gina Schild Knowles's uncle

Jerry Schild began racing at Meyer Speedway in Houston in 1972. Just two years later, in 1974, he made five starts in NASCAR's top series, finishing eighth at Darlington Raceway, the high point of his brief top level career.

He returned to short track racing in Texas for decades afterward, winning a state championship in 2003 and a Houston Motorsports Park track championship in 2006. In 2009, only weeks after donating a kidney to his sister Linda, he returned to racing. He died in 2012 in Tioga, North Dakota. Press coverage at the time, including the Williston Herald, reported his death as a shooting, and people who knew him in Texas racing were quoted remembering him as a local legend of the sport.
