Family
Wife of Hermann Schild; mother of Erwin, Kurt, and Margot Schild

Hetti Schild, born Hetti Neugarten, was the mother of Erwin, Kurt, and Margot Schild, and the wife of Hermann Schild, in Cologne Mülheim.

In 1941, Hetti and Hermann were deported to the Riga ghetto. She did not survive. No confirmed photograph of Hetti Schild has been located for this archive, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
