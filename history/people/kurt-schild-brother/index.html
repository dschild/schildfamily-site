Kurt Schild

Cologne Mülheim, Germany · Later United States

Wider Schild Story

Presented as wider Schild surname history connected to a documented German family, not a confirmed direct ancestor.

Family
Older brother of Erwin Schild; son of Hermann and Hetti Schild
Years
Emigrated to the United States in 1940

Kurt Schild was Erwin Schild’s older brother. One account describes him as among the last Jews able to leave Germany, emigrating to the United States in 1940.

Kurt later served in the American military. No confirmed photograph of Kurt Schild has been located for this archive, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
