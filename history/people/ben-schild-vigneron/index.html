Ben Schild

Settled the Barossa in 1952 · Died 1956 · Vigneron, Rowland Flat, South Australia

Wider Schild Story

Presented as wider Schild surname history connected to the Barossa Valley wine story, not a confirmed direct ancestor.

Family
Husband of Alma Schild; father of Ed Schild

Ben Schild, with his wife Alma, purchased and settled a property along historic Narrow Road near Rowland Flat in the Barossa Valley in 1952. This is the earliest documented moment in the family’s Barossa vineyard story.

Ben died unexpectedly in 1956. His son Ed, then sixteen and the youngest of eight children, took over the property and went on to lead its expansion over the following decades. No confirmed photograph of Ben Schild has been located for this archive, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
