Family
Son of Ben and Alma Schild; father of Michael Schild; grandfather of Angus Schild

Ed Schild, son of Ben and Alma Schild, was the youngest of eight children and the only son still living at home when his father died unexpectedly in 1956. Ed took over the family property at sixteen years old.

Over the following decades, Ed led the expansion of the family’s vineyard holdings across the Southern Barossa, including the purchase of Angus Brae in December 2001, named for his grandson Angus. Around 2022, as the family shifted its focus toward grapegrowing, Ed stepped back from leading the business and his son Michael took on that role. No confirmed photograph of Ed Schild has been located for this archive, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
