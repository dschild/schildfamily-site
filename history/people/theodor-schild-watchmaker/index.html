Theodor Schild

1870–1950 · Watch Manufacturer, Grenchen, Switzerland

Family
Son of Urs Schild

Theodor Schild, a son of Urs Schild, took charge of the family watch business in 1899, after his brother Max left the company the year before. Theodor led the firm for more than three decades, until 1932.

Under his leadership, the company moved firmly into the emerging wristwatch market, patented a protective watch lug in 1904, and saw the Eterna trademark registered and put into public use starting in 1905. No authenticated portrait of Theodor Schild has been located for this archive, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
