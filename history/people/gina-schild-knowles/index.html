Daughter of Larry Schild; Jerry Schild's niece

Gina Schild Knowles took over the lease of Houston Motorsports Park in 2019, the same track where her uncle Jerry had won a championship in 2006. Under her, the track worked its way back to NASCAR sanctioning around 2020.

She represents this family's shift from driving to stewarding the sport itself, working alongside race director Mason Mitchell to keep the track running.
