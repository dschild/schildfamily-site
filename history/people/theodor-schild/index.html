Theodor Schild

b. c. 1818, Saxony, Germany · Farmer & Early Grapetown Settler

Born
c. 1818, Saxony, Germany
Occupation
Farmer
Places
Grapetown, Fredericksburg, Texas
Land
Received a deed near Grapetown, Feb. 13, 1854

Theodor Schild was among the early settlers around Grapetown, Texas, a small German community a few miles from Fredericksburg, and received a land deed there on February 13, 1854. The 1860 U.S. Census for Fredericksburg, Gillespie County records him about age 42, born in Saxony, Germany, and working as a farmer.

Immediately adjacent in that same census is August Schild — also Saxony-born and close in age — though whether the two men were related has not been established (see below).

A Family Mystery

Were August and Theodor Schild brothers?

They appear beside one another in the 1860 census, were close in age, and were both born in Saxony, but their exact relationship has not yet been proven.
