Angus Schild is a grandson of Ed Schild. The Angus Brae vineyard, purchased by the family north of Lyndoch in December 2001, was named for him, combined with the Scottish word “brae,” meaning a steep hillside.

Little else about Angus Schild is documented in sources available to this archive. No confirmed photograph has been located, so this page uses a plain initials portrait rather than an invented image.
