History/People/Urs Schild

US

Urs Schild

1829–1888 · Watch Movement Manufacturer, Grenchen, Switzerland

Wider Schild Story

Presented as wider Schild surname history in Swiss watchmaking, not a confirmed direct ancestor.

Years
1829–1888
Role
Watch Movement Manufacturer
Place
Grenchen, Switzerland
Company
Founded the business that became Eterna

Urs Schild began as a schoolteacher. In the early 1850s he moved into the watch trade taking hold in and around Grenchen, and on November 7, 1856, he and Dr Joseph Girard, a physician, founded a small business making watch movements there.

Around 1866, Dr Girard retired and Urs Schild took full ownership of the business. He steadily built it up, bringing in water and steam powered machinery, and by the late 1870s the company had begun producing complete watches, not only the movements inside them. When Urs Schild died in 1888, the business passed to his brother Adolf and his sons Max and Theodor, who carried it forward into the twentieth century as Eterna.

No authenticated portrait of Urs Schild has been located for this archive. Rather than substitute an invented image, this page uses a plain initials portrait, in keeping with the rest of this site.
